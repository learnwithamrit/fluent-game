English Idioms

Idioms are expressions that mean something different from the literal words. Master them and sound like a natural English speaker.

📖 5 min read 🎯 Intermediate 📌 10 examples

📌 What is an Idiom?

An idiom is a group of words whose meaning is completely different from the individual words. For example, "kick the bucket" has nothing to do with kicking or buckets — it means to die.

Native English speakers use idioms constantly in conversation, books, films, and news. Understanding them is one of the fastest ways to improve your comprehension and sound more natural.

💡 You cannot translate idioms word for word. Always learn the full expression as one unit, along with its meaning and a sample sentence.

💬 10 Common Idioms

Here are ten everyday English idioms you will hear in real conversations. Study the meaning and usage sentence for each one.

Break the ice
To do or say something to relieve tension or awkwardness in a social situation.
"The teacher told a joke to break the ice on the first day of class."
বাংলা: সম্পর্ক বা পরিস্থিতি স্বাভাবিক করা
Hit the nail on the head
To describe exactly what is causing a situation or problem.
"You really hit the nail on the head when you said the problem is communication."
বাংলা: একদম সঠিক কথা বলা
Under the weather
Feeling slightly ill or unwell.
"I won't be coming to school today — I'm feeling a bit under the weather."
বাংলা: একটু অসুস্থ বোধ করা

🎯 How to Learn Idioms Effectively

"Don't just memorise the meaning — use it in a sentence you could say today."

The biggest mistake students make is writing down idioms without ever using them. Here is a simple method that works:

  1. 1 Read the idiom and its meaning carefully. Make sure you understand the context it is used in.
  2. 2 Say the example sentence out loud three times. Your mouth needs to practise, not just your eyes.
  3. 3 Write your own sentence using the idiom — about your school, family, or daily life. Personal sentences stick in memory much longer.
  4. 4 Try to use it in conversation within 24 hours. Even if you make a mistake, that is how fluency builds.
📝 Keep a small notebook just for idioms. Write the idiom, meaning, and one personal sentence. Review it every few days.
